Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

Who we are

WePickThePic is developed and published by Sergiu Martin, Romania. For any privacy question or request, contact us at smartin.appsupport@gmail.com.

In short

WePickThePic has no sign-up, no cloud, no servers, no ads and no tracking, and no analytics of its own. Your photos are analysed on your iPhone and never uploaded to us. We never see them, and we don’t collect any data about you.

Your photos

WePickThePic asks for access to your photo library so it can find your trips and pick the best photos. Photos are analysed on your iPhone with Apple’s built-in tools. They are not copied into the app or sent anywhere for analysis.

To work faster next time, WePickThePic keeps the results of the analysis on your iPhone: which photos belong to a trip and their scores, never copies of the photos. It also keeps your choices (photos you left out or added back), what it learned about your taste, the trips’ names and your settings. All of this stays in the app’s private storage on your iPhone.

Where Apple Intelligence is turned on, WePickThePic also asks Apple’s model on your iPhone whether a photo looks like part of the trip and which of similar shots is best. This runs on the iPhone only: no cloud model, nothing is uploaded.

If a photo is only in iCloud and your iPhone has no copy of it, WePickThePic asks iOS for a small copy from your own iCloud to look at, under Apple’s privacy policy. Nothing is uploaded.

Albums you save

When you save an album, WePickThePic creates it in your Photos library, next to your photos, with your photos in it. WePickThePic never deletes a photo. If you use iCloud Photos, your albums are synced by Apple like any other album, under Apple’s privacy policy.

Place names

To name your trips (“Lisbon, Sintra”), WePickThePic asks Apple’s map services for the name of a place from a photo’s location. Only the location is sent, never the photo, and Apple handles it under its own privacy policy. Without a connection, WePickThePic still finds your trips and picks the photos; only the place names may be missing.

Making an album together

In short: photos go directly between your phones, encrypted. Nothing is uploaded.

When you make an album with friends, their iPhones join yours with a 6-digit code that your iPhone shows. From the code, the phones agree on a key that only they know; the code itself is never sent. The phones then connect directly to each other (peer-to-peer Wi-Fi, as AirDrop does, or the same Wi-Fi network); on iPhone this needs the Local Network permission. While the session is open, your iPhone announces itself only under a random name, without your name or any photos.

Your iPhone and your friends’ exchange only what’s needed to make the album: the name you choose to show (optional), information about the photos (when they were taken and how similar they are) and the photos chosen for the album. Everything is encrypted with the session’s key. The phone that started the album passes photos on between the others, so each friend gets the album’s photos; there is no WePickThePic server in between. Photos your friends receive are saved in their own Photos library and are theirs to keep.

If a photo is only in iCloud, your iPhone may download it from your own iCloud first, under Apple’s privacy policy.

Purchases

WePickThePic Pro is bought once through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether Pro has been purchased on your Apple Account.

To count the free albums, WePickThePic keeps two numbers (albums made and sessions started) in your iPhone’s keychain, so they stay when you reinstall the app. If iCloud Keychain is on, Apple syncs them to your other devices, end-to-end encrypted. They never include photos or anything about them.

How your data is protected

Everything WePickThePic keeps stays in the app’s private storage, which other apps can’t read, and is protected by your iPhone’s own security, such as device encryption and your passcode. What goes between iPhones when you make an album together is encrypted (see above).

Test reports

WePickThePic’s test tools can create a report about one trip (scores, groups and picks, no photos). It leaves your iPhone only if you share it yourself.

Support emails

WePickThePic keeps a short technical log on your iPhone: the app version, the iOS version, which screens were opened, the steps of an analysis or of making an album together, and errors. It never contains your photos, names, album titles or places. It leaves your iPhone only if you choose “Include diagnostic info” when you write to support from Settings, and you see it in the email before you send it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Data retention and deletion

What WePickThePic keeps stays on your iPhone for as long as you keep the app; we never have a copy. Uninstalling WePickThePic removes it. Albums you saved stay in Photos: delete them there if you want. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing we could access, correct or delete on request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app and on our website with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to smartin.appsupport@gmail.com.
