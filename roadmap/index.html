What’s coming

WePickThePic is made by one person, and most of the changes so far came from people testing it on real trips.

These are the things we're working on and thinking about. They are ideas, not promises: there are no dates, and some may change or never come.

New features, same promise: your photos stay on your iPhone and your friends' phones. No account, no ads, nothing uploaded.

What would help you most?

Tell us: in WePickThePic, go to Settings › Contact support, or email smartin.appsupport@gmail.com.

Being tested now

In the next test version on TestFlight:

  • WePickThePic Pro: bought once, no subscription, for as many albums as you like. WePickThePic stays free to download and to try, and joining a friend's album is always free.
  • Once, after your second album, WePickThePic may ask you to rate it in the App Store.
  • An Open Settings button when WePickThePic needs a permission you turned off, so you can turn it back on in one tap.
  • Help and What's new in Settings.

Ideas we're looking at

  • Join a friend's album by scanning a QR code, instead of typing the code.
  • Friends with an Android phone can join your album and share their photos.
  • Add the trip's videos to the album.
  • Fix a found trip yourself: merge two trips, split one, or add and remove photos.
  • Add new photos later to an album you made together.
  • Turn an album into a slideshow or a PDF, made on your iPhone.

What changed so far: see What’s new.
