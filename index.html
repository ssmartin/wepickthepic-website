The best photos of your trip, picked on your iPhone. WePickThePic has no servers, needs no sign-up and collects no data: your photos are never uploaded, and nothing is ever deleted.

Hundreds of photos. One album.

Hundreds of holiday photos, ten shots of the same view, the good ones lost among them. WePickThePic groups the similar shots, keeps the best of each and makes one album of the trip, day by day, saved in Apple Photos.

✨

Picks the best

  • Similar shots are grouped and the best one is kept: sharp, eyes open, faces looking good
  • Every pick is explained in plain words, like "Best of 4 similar shots: sharpest, faces look best"
  • Asks you only when it's a close call
  • Check what was left out, compare it with the album and swap in any photo you like more
  • Learns from your choices: the shot you keep, the photos you add or leave out, even the kinds of scenes you like. What it learns stays on your iPhone
  • Your favourites are always kept
  • Choose the album size: S, M or L
🗺️

Finds your trips

  • Finds your trips from your photos' dates and places (optional)
  • Or start from dates, one of your albums, or photos you choose
  • Works with limited photo access: WePickThePic sees only the photos you allow
🛡️

Nothing deleted

  • WePickThePic never deletes a photo. The album only points to your photos: nothing is copied or moved
  • Delete the album any time; your photos stay where they were

Travelled with friends?

Make the album together: everyone's iPhones bring their photos, and WePickThePic picks the best of all of them. Phone to phone, encrypted, with no cloud in between.

  • Up to 5 iPhones make one album: friends join with a 6-digit code
  • The phones connect directly when they are close: no shared Wi-Fi needed
  • Best of the trip, or everyone's photos without the duplicates
  • Everyone chooses what they share, down to single photos, and gets the same album
  • Joining is free

Private by design

  • No sign-up, no account, no servers: there is nothing to sign in to
  • No cloud: your photos are analysed on your iPhone and never uploaded. We never see them
  • No ads, no tracking, no data collection
  • Photos go directly between your phones, encrypted. Nothing is uploaded
  • Nothing is kept on our side: your choices stay on your iPhone, and your photos are never copied
  • Reset what WePickThePic learned any time in Settings
