Support

We’re happy to help.

Contact

The quickest way: in WePickThePic, go to Settings › Contact support and choose to include the diagnostic info: the app version, your iPhone model and what the app did recently. It never contains your photos or their places, you see all of it in the email before you send it, and it helps us find the problem much faster.

Can’t open the app? Email smartin.appsupport@gmail.com and mention your iPhone model and iOS version.

Questions

Does WePickThePic upload my photos?

No. WePickThePic has no cloud and no servers. Photos are analysed on your iPhone and never uploaded to us; we never see them. Only when you make an album together do photos go to your friends' iPhones, directly and encrypted.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up.

Will WePickThePic delete photos?

Never. WePickThePic only adds: it saves the album in Photos, next to your photos. Photos you leave out of an album stay in your library.

How does WePickThePic choose?

It groups similar shots and keeps the best one of each group: sharp, well exposed, eyes open. When two shots are close, it asks you. It learns from your choices, so its picks get closer to your taste over time.

WePickThePic doesn't find my trip.

WePickThePic looks for days with photos far enough from home. In Create, tap + and choose Dates to set the trip's days yourself, An album to start from one of your albums in Photos, or Photos to choose them by hand.

WePickThePic can't see my photos.

WePickThePic needs access to your photo library: Settings › Apps › WePickThePic › Photos › Full Access. With "Limited Access" it only sees the photos you selected.

Some photos couldn't be added or saved.

A photo that is only in iCloud needs internet to download first, and saving needs free space on the iPhone. WePickThePic says how many photos couldn't be added; try again when the iPhone is online and has space.

Making an album together

How do my friends join?

Everyone needs WePickThePic. In Create, tap the trip and choose Me and friends nearby; your iPhone shows a 6-digit code. Your friends open the Join tab and type the code. Joining is free.

Do we need the same Wi-Fi?

No. The iPhones connect directly to each other, as AirDrop does. They need to be near each other, with Wi-Fi turned on (no network needed), and WePickThePic open on every phone.

My friend can't find my iPhone.

Check that WePickThePic may use the local network: Settings › Apps › WePickThePic › Local Network. Keep WePickThePic open on both phones and the phones unlocked. If it still fails, close the session and start a new one with a new code.

Who gets the album?

Everyone. Your iPhone collects the photos chosen for the album and offers each friend the ones they don't have yet. Each friend saves the album in their own Photos.

Language

WePickThePic follows your iPhone's language (English, German, Romanian, Spanish). To use another one just for WePickThePic: Settings › Apps › WePickThePic › Language.
