What’s new

Updated 27 November 2026

What changed in WePickThePic, newest first. WePickThePic is being tested on TestFlight; version 1.0 is coming to the App Store, and many of the changes below came from testing it on real trips.

Update for build 11 – 27 November 2026

  • Tap the number of photos picked to see what the trip's photos are: different moments, similar photos, screenshots and documents.
  • Apple Intelligence no longer leaves out sharp photos as "blurry": the app's own sharpness measurement has to agree.
  • Settings › Language: use the iPhone's language, or choose English, Deutsch, Română or Español just for WePickThePic.
  • Apple Intelligence no longer leaves out a photo taken among the day's others, like an elephant on the day at the zoo, also from a phone with location off.
  • Close on similar photos or a comparison brings you straight back to the photo you came from; keeping or swapping brings you back to the album.
  • An album photo offers one choice: its similar photos, or else the photos left out from the same time.

Build 11 – 27 November 2026

  • When two photos taken around the same time are about as good, Apple Intelligence picks the one that shows the trip better, and says why.
  • Tap an album photo to see the photos it was chosen over at that time, and compare them one by one. "The only photo from that time" when nothing else was taken then.

Update for build 10 – 27 November 2026

  • Of photos taken around the same time, the better ones get into the album, whatever order you took them in. Before, the first one could keep out a better one.
  • Comparing a left-out photo with the album says why in plain words, or that they are about as good.

Update for build 10 – 26 November 2026

  • On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, the album now shows after a short step, "Fine-tuning with Apple Intelligence", so it no longer changes while you look at it. "Show the album now" skips the wait.

Update for build 10 – 25 November 2026

  • Making an album together: the two choices are now "Everyone's best" and "Everyone's photos", so it's clear both use everyone's photos.

Build 10 – 24 November 2026

  • While a trip is being named, its card says "Finding a name…".
  • Trip names use the places in them: the town where most photos were taken, two towns ("Oradea & Băile Felix"), or the country, like "Switzerland, Summer 2026". Only when the photos are spread out, just the season.
  • WePickThePic Pro, bought once: 3 albums are free, then Pro makes as many as you like. Friends can always join for free.
  • A new app icon: a trip under a blue sky.
  • Settings look cleaner, with Rate the app, Help and What's new.
  • When a permission is missing, a button opens the iPhone's Settings right there.
  • A short message confirms things like hiding a trip or resetting your taste.
  • The cover of a trip you haven't opened yet shows your favourite photos from across the trip.

Update for build 9 – 22 November 2026

  • A new album size XL, about 18 photos a day. M and L are a bit bigger: about 6 and 12 a day.
  • Comparing a left-out photo with the album says why the album photo is in, like "a better overall look, you took 2 photos of that moment".
  • Clear trip names: the town where most photos were taken, or the season, like "Summer 2026", instead of a list of streets. Day lines show towns only.
  • A trip's card shows photos from across the trip, not the same scene three times.
  • Closing a long list of left-out photos keeps you where you were.
  • Photos with no similar ones say so: "The only photo of this moment".

Update for build 9 – 21 November 2026

  • Tap a photo in the album, then "See similar photos": the photos it was picked from, to keep it or swap it for another.
  • Big trips: scrolling the album no longer jumps between days or shows empty space.

Build 9 – 21 November 2026

  • A new app icon: everyone's photos, the best one with a coral star, on space grey. The start screen matches.
  • Big trips keep being picked when you leave the app (iOS 26 and later): your iPhone shows the progress and lets you stop it there.
  • Better album names from Apple Intelligence: named after the places where you took the most photos, or after the season, like "Summer 2026 – Best of", when you travelled around.
  • Photos that are only in iCloud are no longer blurry in the grids: a sharp copy comes from your own iCloud.

Update for build 8 – 21 November 2026

  • Big trips: scrolling past a long list of left-out photos no longer makes the screen jump.
  • The same picture twice (for example an edited copy) no longer ends up in the album twice.
  • Choosing between similar photos: the photo is much bigger.
  • "Our pick" is shown once, with the same words everywhere.
  • The pencil to rename a trip is bigger and sits right after its name.

Build 8 – 21 November 2026

  • Photos that are only in iCloud are picked as fairly as the rest: when your iPhone has no copy, a small one comes from your own iCloud to look at.
  • Drawings, cartoons and clip art are recognised as not part of the trip.
  • Settings shows the exact version, to help when you contact support.

Updates for build 7 – 21 November 2026

  • What you leave out of an album is remembered: it isn't suggested again in later albums unless you add it back.
  • Making an album together says plainly how photos travel: directly between your phones, encrypted, nothing uploaded.
  • Friends' photos show a spinner until they've arrived, instead of a black tile.
  • Joining a friend's album shows four steps at the top, and Done takes you to Albums, where the newest album is first.
  • Apple Intelligence's album name becomes the album's title, unless you named it yourself.
  • Change selection opens an album as it was saved, right away.

Build 7 – 20 November 2026

  • Apple Intelligence helps pick (where it's turned on): photos that don't belong to the trip, and blurry or accidental shots, stay out of the album; it decides close calls between similar photos and suggests a name. Everything it leaves out is still under Left out, to add back.
  • Friends' photos are sharp, like your own.

Builds 4 and 5 – 20 November 2026

  • Tapping a trip asks who adds photos: Just me, or Me and friends nearby.
  • Making an album together: the code shows right away, and photos received from friends keep the date and place they were taken.
  • Albums: swipe a card to hide it; albums made together can be changed later.
  • Fixed photos and messages getting lost between the phones.

Build 3 – 19 November 2026

  • A new name: WePickThePic. Everyone takes pictures. We pick the best.
  • Joining a friend's album: choose other photos than your friend's days, from dates, an album or single photos.
  • On the host's iPhone: four steps at the top, a ✓ for each friend whose photos are in, and a warning before leaving anyone out.
  • Pick the dates of a trip from a calendar.
  • Contact support can include diagnostic info (never your photos or places).

Build 2 – 19 November 2026

The first test version on TestFlight.

  • Make an album together with up to 4 friends nearby: the phones talk directly, nothing goes through the internet.
  • WePickThePic picks the best photo of each moment, explains why in plain words, and learns your taste on your iPhone.
  • See what was left out of each day, with the reason, and add any photo back with one tap. Your favourites are always in.
  • Albums of size S, M or L, saved in Photos as "… – Best of". Nothing is ever deleted.
  • In English, German, Romanian and Spanish, with light and dark appearance.
